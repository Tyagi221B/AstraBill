# myBillBook

## Overview

A direct, well-funded, mobile + web competitor in the Indian SME billing
space. Their landing claims **"Trusted by 1 Crore+ Businesses"** — likely
inflated (signup ≠ active), but the floor is genuine: tens of lakhs of
active SMEs. Domain: **mybillbook.in**. Run by FloBiz (YC W19, ~$40M
raised across rounds). They show up alongside Vyapar in every Indian
billing-software comparison.

## Their hero pitch (verbatim from landing, May 2026)

> Best GST Billing Software for Small Business in India
>
> - Create GST bill in **8 seconds**
> - Increase stock rotation **2.8x faster**
> - Collect **97% payments on time**

Below the fold: a "2026 Product Tour" video, plus four trust laurels:

- Best Tech Brands — ET Now 2025
- Excellence in GST — Business Connect 2025
- 4.7★ Google Play
- 100% Data Privacy — CA Trusted

## The differentiation they lead with — and the lesson for AstraBill

Their second-most-prominent section is **"Your bill, Your Brand: Color · Logo · Font · And More!"** with an animated word swap. The hero shows three template cards side-by-side (Thermal · A4 · A5 toggle) and a "Customize your own Invoice" interactive canvas with drag-toggleable columns.

**The point:** myBillBook treats *invoice design + brand customization*
as their #1 paid-tier upsell. It's not vanity — it's what convinces a
shop owner that "this invoice will represent **my** shop, not look like
generic software output". The competitive bar AstraBill must clear is
not "we render a PDF" — it's "your customer sees your invoice and knows
it's *yours*".

This bar is **not** currently met by AstraBill (Phase 1 + 2A ship one
template, one font, no brand colour, no logo upload). Phase 2C plans
the response — see [[decision/invoice-design-system]].

## Template catalogue (observed on their landing)

- **Standard A4 GST** — a relatively clean serif template; ornate
  gold-leaf corners for some businesses
- **Pharma / batch-tracking template** — HSN + BATCH + EXP DATE + MFG
  DATE columns visible (the Parshwanath Medicare screenshot)
- **A5 / receipt format**
- **Thermal 80mm** — POS-printer-sized condensed format
- **"Special Themes"** — including a Ganesha-watermarked variant with
  Varanasi ghat imagery. Religious/cultural themed templates are a
  *real* product feature for Indian shop owners, not kitsch.
- **Customize your own** — a builder canvas where the owner toggles
  column visibility (HSN, Batch No., Exp Date, MFG Date, Quantity,
  Price/Item, Discount, Total), uploads logo, names business

## Strengths

- **Hero loop is bill-creation, not feature-listing.** "8 seconds to
  GST bill" is concrete + verifiable. AstraBill's "It's that simple"
  is good but vaguer — they back it up with a quantified time-to-bill.
- **Trust laurels.** ET Now / Business Connect / 4.7★ / CA Trusted is
  a tight social-proof strip. Indian buyers heavily weight third-party
  validation.
- **Invoice template library** is their actual moat against Vyapar —
  more polished templates, more themed variants (festive, sector-
  specific).
- **Stock rotation framing.** Marketing inventory as "2.8x faster
  rotation" is sharper than Tally/Vyapar's generic "track stock". It
  ties inventory to **cash flow**, the metric SMEs feel daily.
- **Video product tour.** A founder/employee in shirt explaining the
  product on the hero — humanises the software, lowers the "scary
  SaaS" barrier for first-gen digital users.

## Weaknesses

- **Generic positioning.** They sell "GST billing software" — same
  category as Vyapar, Tally, Marg. No sharp wedge.
- **WhatsApp is not their hero.** Despite WhatsApp being the obvious
  Indian SME channel, myBillBook's marketing keeps it a sub-feature.
  AstraBill leads with WhatsApp — that's the difference.
- **UPI payment-link reconciliation** isn't prominent on their
  landing. They have payments but don't bet on it as the wedge.
- **Customization complexity.** The "drag your columns" builder is
  powerful but, for the half of SMEs who'll never touch it, it's
  intimidating. AstraBill can win by shipping 3 great defaults
  instead of one generic-plus-builder.
- **Backed by FloBiz / VC funding.** Burns capital on category
  competition; needs scale to justify the round. Slower iteration on
  positioning.

## Pricing (snapshot, May 2026)

myBillBook prices on a "Start Free Billing" + "Book Free Demo" funnel.
Promotional banner says "Save upto 65% today" — they're aggressively
discounting, which signals high churn / low close rates at sticker
price.

A Pro/Diamond tier is gated by demo. Confirmed paid plans land in the
₹999 – ₹2,499/month range (matches public listings on competitor-
comparison sites). Annual billing preferred.

## Threat assessment

**High.** They're the closest direct competitor:

- Same buyer (Indian SME, trading + retail)
- Same channels (Google Search, Play Store, sales calls)
- Same paid range (₹5k – ₹20k/year)
- More polished templates than Vyapar
- VC-funded — can outspend us on Google Ads

**Where AstraBill must win:**

1. **WhatsApp loop quality** (their weakest spot)
2. **Working UPI reconciliation** (their landing doesn't lead with it)
3. **Invoice design that doesn't feel like a builder** — three great
   defaults, brand the way myBillBook lets users brand, but without
   the column-toggle complexity for V1
4. **Speed of bill creation** — beat their "8 seconds" claim with a
   product-keyboard-friendly flow

## Why this competitor was added late

Phase 1 wiki research surveyed Tally, Vyapar, KhataBook, Zoho Books,
Refrens, Odoo. myBillBook wasn't initially scoped because their public
brand recognition is lower than Vyapar's. The competitor screenshot
ingest of 2026-05-19 (the brand-customization hero) made clear they
are a peer-level threat — added then.
