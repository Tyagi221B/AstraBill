# Refrens

## Overview

AI Accounting and ERP platform. Originally freelancer-focused, now expanding to SMEs. **150K+ global users**, **4.8★** from 11,700+ reviews. India-built product with strong local compliance. Growing fast.

## Pricing

- Free plan (limited)
- Paid: starts at **₹1,600/month**

## Strengths

- WhatsApp invoice delivery already built in
- Automated payment reminders (WhatsApp + email)
- Time tracking + invoicing together (good for agencies/consultants)
- Multi-currency
- Custom invoice templates, formulas, colors
- GST, e-invoices, credit/debit notes
- Quotations, proforma, delivery notes — full document suite
- 4.8★ = best product ratings of any competitor
- India-specific features done well

## Weaknesses / Where It Falls Short for AstraBill's Target

- **Positioned for freelancers and agencies** — not kirana/retail/traditional SME
- ₹1,600/month is expensive for Tier 2/3 SME
- Less inventory management depth
- No POS-style billing for counter use
- Less brand recognition vs Vyapar/KhataBook in Tier 2/3

## Strategic Signal

Refrens is our closest product-philosophy competitor (modern, WhatsApp-first, India-built). They've nailed the freelancer segment. The gap: **traditional SME** (shops, contractors, service businesses) is underserved. These users don't relate to Refrens's freelancer positioning. AstraBill can own that positioning.

**Worth learning from**: their 4.8★ is the highest in the space — study what they do right on UX.
