# Tally / TallyPrime

## Overview

The incumbent. Controls **70-90% of India's MSME accounting market** and has for 3 decades. ~2.5 million businesses worldwide. The brother's 24k/yr pain is real and representative.

## Pricing (2025)

| Plan | One-Time (Lifetime) | Annual TSS Renewal |
|------|--------------------|--------------------|
| Silver (1 user) | ₹22,500 + 18% GST = **~₹26,550** | ₹4,500 + GST = ~₹5,310/yr |
| Gold (multi-user) | ₹67,500 + 18% GST = **~₹79,650** | ₹13,500 + GST = ~₹15,930/yr |
| TallyPrime Server | ₹2,70,000 + GST | — |

- Prices increased ~25% in Feb 2024
- Without TSS renewal: license still works but no GST updates or connected services → effectively forces renewal
- Total annual cost for a Silver user = **~₹5,310/yr renewal** (but if buying new: ~₹26,550 upfront + ~₹5,310/yr)
- Brother paying "24k" — likely a combination or an older quote, now higher

## Core Problems (Why SMEs Leave)

| Problem | Impact |
|---------|--------|
| **Desktop-only** | No mobile access, stuck at the office PC, can't check reports on the go |
| **No cloud** | Must be present, no remote work, no multi-location |
| **Outdated UI** | Looks like 1998, intimidating for non-accountants |
| **Steep learning curve** | Business owners need dedicated training; can't self-onboard |
| **No real-time collaboration** | One ledger at a time, single screen per transaction |
| **Limited mobile** | No mobile-optimized version |
| **GST updates require TSS** | Held hostage to annual renewal just to stay compliant |

## Strengths (Why They Still Dominate)

- Accountants / CAs know Tally — switching means retraining your CA
- Feature-complete for any business type
- Trusted brand, 30 years
- Works offline (no internet dependency — paradoxically a strength in rural India)
- Inventory, payroll, manufacturing — everything is there

## Strategic Signal

Tally's strength IS the switching cost: CA familiarity. AstraBill doesn't need to replace Tally — it needs to win the businesses that **haven't adopted Tally yet** (first-time software buyers in Tier 2/3) or those **frustrated with Tally's desktop-lock**.
