# Zoho Books

## Overview

The most feature-complete cloud billing/accounting for Indian SMEs. Zoho is a Indian company (Chennai) with global reach. Zoho Books competes globally with QuickBooks and FreshBooks while having India-specific compliance built in.

## Pricing (India, 2026)

| Plan | Price/month |
|------|------------|
| Free | ₹0 (businesses under ₹25L annual revenue, 1 user, 1000 invoices/year) |
| Standard | ₹899/month |
| Professional | ₹1,499/month |
| Premium | ₹2,999/month |
| Elite | ₹5,999/month |
| Ultimate | ₹9,999/month |

## Strengths

- Best-in-class cloud accounting — GST, e-invoicing, bank reconciliation, TDS
- India-built = deep GST and compliance support
- Multi-currency (~100 currencies)
- API-first — integrates with everything
- Free plan exists (but limited)
- Rating: 4.6★

## Critical Weakness: Complexity

- **Setup requires expertise**: GST config, chart of accounts, HSN/SAC codes, bank feeds, TDS rates all need correct initial configuration
- Wrong setup = cascading compliance consequences
- Many SME owners can't self-onboard — they need a CA or Zoho consultant
- This is the gap: **it's too good for what a simple SME needs**
- Zoho Invoice (their standalone invoicing product) cannot be integrated with Zoho Inventory — a surprising limitation
- The free plan has no bank connections or transaction imports

## Who It's Actually For

Zoho Books is excellent for businesses that have a semi-technical owner or an accountant handling setup. It's overkill for a kirana store or a simple service business. The complexity is a moat against migration IN, but also against adoption by first-time software buyers.

## Strategic Signal

AstraBill is NOT competing with Zoho Books at launch. We're fishing in the pool of businesses that find Zoho Books too complex or too expensive. The framing: "Zoho Books for your accountant; AstraBill for you."
