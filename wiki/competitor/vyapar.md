# Vyapar

## Overview

The most credible mobile-first challenger to Tally. **1 crore+ users** (10M+). Built for Indian GST compliance. Raised **$35.6M**. Rating: 4.7★. The one we most need to understand because it's closest to what AstraBill will be.

## Pricing

- Free plan: 1 user, basic features
- Paid: ~₹399-599/month (or ~₹4,800-7,200/year)
- Desktop + Android app

## Strengths

- GST-ready invoicing, inventory, accounting
- Multi-user login
- Works offline
- Barcode integration
- Auto transaction SMS
- Financial accounting with reports
- Strong brand in SME segment (retail, pharmacy, grocery)
- 1 crore users = network effect and trust

## Critical Complaints (Our Opportunity)

These are from verified user reviews — this is where we win:

| Complaint | Severity | AstraBill Opportunity |
|-----------|----------|----------------------|
| **WhatsApp invoice sends blank files** | 🔴 Critical | Fix the most-used share channel |
| **Data loss / data glitches** | 🔴 Critical | Reliability as core promise |
| **Salesman can see purchase prices & profit margins** | 🔴 Critical | Proper RBAC from day 1 |
| **Customer support ignores old customers** | 🟠 High | Responsive support = moat |
| **App hangs during business hours** | 🟠 High | Performance-first PWA |
| **Limited/inflexible user roles** | 🟠 High | Granular RBAC |
| **iPhone errors creating/saving invoices** | 🟡 Medium | Web-first solves this |
| **5-year users reporting data mismatches** | 🔴 Critical | Audit log, data integrity |
| **Doesn't listen to user feature requests** | 🟡 Medium | Build feedback loop in product |
| **Customization limited** | 🟡 Medium | Template engine |

## Key Insight on RBAC

A salesman being able to see **profit margins and purchase prices** is a massive privacy/business risk. This is not a feature gap — it's a fundamental trust failure. Indian business owners are extremely sensitive about this (family-run businesses, employees they don't fully trust). AstraBill should treat RBAC as a **first-class product feature** from day 1, not an afterthought.

## Their Funding Trajectory

$35.6M raised. Much less than KhataBook ($211M). Bootstrapped more cautiously. Still growing but may be resource-constrained on product.
