# KhataBook

## Overview

Digital ledger app turned accounting platform. **50M+ downloads**, **10 languages**, $500M valuation (2024), raised **$211.5M**. Revenue: ₹105 Cr FY24. But: **headcount declining 22% YoY** as of Aug 2025 — possible product/business contraction.

## Positioning

Not a billing-first product. KhataBook is primarily a **digital credit/debit ledger** — replacing the physical khata (notebook). It tracks "who owes me money" and "who I owe". Recently expanding into invoicing and accounting, but core identity is ledger.

## Pricing

- Core product: **free** (credit ledger)
- Paid features for invoicing, reports, GST

## Strengths

- 50M downloads — massive top-of-funnel
- 10 Indian languages — Tier 2/3 penetration
- Simple, kirana-friendly UX
- Trust brand in rural/semi-urban India
- Credit ledger is stickier than invoicing (daily habit)
- myBillBook: their dedicated billing product

## Weaknesses

- Core product (ledger) ≠ billing software — limited for real invoicing needs
- No barcode, no advanced inventory
- Feature gaps vs Vyapar
- 22% headcount reduction — product investment may be slowing
- Needs better UPI integration (own complaint from users)
- "Needs features to adapt to changing scenario" — stagnating

## myBillBook (Their Billing Product)

- Stock issue: can generate bills even when stock = 0 (gives false inventory data)
- Salesman can edit prices while invoicing (RBAC gap)
- These bugs suggest they haven't prioritized billing product quality

## Strategic Signal

KhataBook is a **distribution play** (50M users) more than a product excellence play. They've moved into billing (myBillBook) via acquisition and feature addition, not as a focused billing product. The billing product is mediocre. This means their 50M users are potential AstraBill customers who already have the habit but not a good billing solution.
