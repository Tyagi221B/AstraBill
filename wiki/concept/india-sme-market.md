# India SME Market — Overview

## Scale

- **63 million MSMEs** in India
- ~30 million (45-50%) currently use some form of digital billing/accounting
- India accounting software market: ~$640M in 2024 → ~$1.4B by 2033 (9.2% CAGR)
- Global billing/invoicing segment for SMEs: $3.4B → projected $7B by 2035
- Only 18% of MSMEs have accessed digital lending; 13% actively use digital marketing

## The Cash Flow Crisis

This is the core MSME pain — not invoicing itself, but getting paid.

- **₹10.7 lakh crore** locked in delayed payments annually
- **₹7.34 lakh crore** tied up specifically in delayed B2B payments
- Average delay: >90 days (large companies using MSMEs as interest-free lenders)
- 50%+ of MSMEs face cash flow problems threatening operational sustainability
- India loses 15 days of small-business productivity/year just chasing payments
- MSME Samadhaan Act mandates 45-day payment terms but enforcement is weak

## Digital Adoption Wave

- 1.2 billion internet users in India by 2025; **65% growth from Tier 2/3 cities**
- Internet penetration in Tier 2/3 cities growing at **30% annually** (double metros)
- 95% of Indian villages now have 4G/5G
- 60% of India's e-commerce demand from Tier 2/3 cities
- UPI: **13 billion transactions in July 2025 alone**

## Pricing Sensitivity by Segment

| Segment | Willingness to Pay | Notes |
|---------|-------------------|-------|
| Metro enterprises | Moderate | Budgets comparable to global, 20-30% negotiation expected |
| Tier 1 SMEs (Pune, Ahmedabad, Hyd) | High sensitivity | 30-40% below list, mid-market focus |
| Tier 2/3 SMBs | Extreme sensitivity | First-time software buyers, 70-80% below US pricing |

Indian SaaS markets: **60-70% lower willingness to pay than Western markets**. Annual billing preferred over monthly.

## What SME Owners Actually Want (ranked)

1. Fast billing at counter with correct GST auto-calculation
2. Works across devices (Android phone + Windows PC + tablet)
3. GST compliance without needing a CA beside them
4. Affordable — ideally under ₹1,000/month
5. Support available evenings, in their language
6. Mobile access for reports on the go
7. Inventory management integration (critical for kirana, pharmacy, retail)
8. Payment collection built-in (not a separate tool)

## Embedded Finance Opportunity (Future)

- India MSME credit gap: ₹20-25 trillion
- Digital lending to MSMEs grew 68% YoY in FY2023
- ₹2.5 lakh crore disbursed via fintech lenders
- Billing data = credit score proxy → **AstraBill sits on transaction data = future lending product**
- Invoice discounting/factoring: fast-growing, paperless, invoice-linked working capital
