# GST Compliance — The Mandatory Hook

## Why GST Is AstraBill's Forcing Function

GST compliance is not optional for Indian businesses. It's the single biggest reason SMEs are adopting billing software — not because they want to, but because they **have to** or face penalties. This is our acquisition hook.

## Current Compliance Landscape (2026)

| Threshold | Requirement |
|-----------|------------|
| ₹5 crore+ turnover | **Mandatory e-invoicing** |
| ₹10 crore+ turnover | Must upload invoices to IRP within 30 days |
| ₹2 crore+ (expected soon) | E-invoicing threshold being lowered — more SMEs getting pulled in |

- GST Council has met **50+ times in 8 years** — constant rule changes
- From July 2025: returns older than 3 years cannot be filed at all
- Manual error penalty: ₹50,000 per error
- Average annual compliance overhead for manufacturing MSMEs: **₹13-17 lakh** (staff time + CA + opportunity cost)
- 88-90% of MSMEs face systemic filing inefficiencies

## Filing Volume

An average Indian business files:
- GSTR-1 (monthly or quarterly)
- GSTR-3B (monthly or quarterly)
- GSTR-9 (annual)
- Plus: e-way bills, e-invoices, TDS returns

For a solo business owner, this is crushing without software.

## The CA Dependency Problem

Most SMEs rely on a CA or accountant to handle GST filing. This creates:
1. **Cost**: CA charges ₹2K-₹10K/month for filing support
2. **Friction**: SME owner doesn't understand their own financials
3. **Delay**: Data goes to CA → CA files → SME gets reports late
4. **Lock-in**: Switching software means retraining the CA too

AstraBill opportunity: **reduce CA dependency** by making GST filing so simple the owner can do it themselves, or at minimum give them real-time visibility without waiting for the CA.

## What Software Must Do

- Auto-calculate GST (CGST, SGST, IGST) on every invoice based on HSN/SAC code
- Generate GSTR-1-ready reports (or direct filing integration)
- E-invoice generation and IRP upload (for businesses above threshold)
- E-way bill integration
- Credit note / debit note support
- Handle multiple GST rates (0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28%) in the same invoice
- Reverse charge mechanism support
- TDS deduction support

## Razorpay's Intelligence (Fix My Itch)

From Razorpay's crowd-sourced problem list:
- **Invoice Management Efficiency** (Itch Score 67.5): "Micro-SMEs spend excessive time on invoice processing without automated solutions."

This is validated — the market wants automation.

## The Opportunity

Most existing apps (Vyapar, KhataBook) handle basic GST invoicing. The gap is:
1. Automated GSTR reconciliation (catch errors before penalty)
2. AI-powered HSN code suggestions (stop SMEs from picking the wrong tax rate)
3. Proactive alerts when rules change
4. One-click filing (direct integration with GST portal via API)
